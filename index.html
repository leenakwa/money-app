STORAGE & BACKUPS

Your data

Purchases, plans, and your saved items stay in this browser. They are not sent to GitHub or any other server.

There is no automatic sync between devices. Clearing site data or closing a private browsing session can remove your records. Keep regular backups.

Save a backup

One JSON file with all your dates, purchases, plans, and saved items.

Restore or transfer

Backed-up records are merged with your current data. Matching IDs are not duplicated; the most recently updated version wins. Deleted records in an older backup will return.

How the budget works

For a month with N days, the budget is €5 × N + €100. The daily limit is that whole monthly budget divided by N. For example, a 30-day month has a €250.00 budget and a daily limit of approximately €8.33.

A Monday–Sunday week gets the sum of its seven daily limits. A week spanning two months uses each date’s own monthly rate. February and leap years are handled automatically.

The daily limit is fixed for that month: it does not increase or decrease after a purchase. Unspent money still counts towards the remaining weekly and monthly budgets, but is not carried into the next month. Monthly summaries include every entry in that calendar month, not just the displayed week.

Plans are not spending. Grey forecasts show what would be left after buying every planned item in the relevant day, week, or month. Negative values mean you would go over budget.

Purchase prices are stored in whole eurocents. Budget calculations keep full precision; only displayed values are rounded to two decimal places. A nonzero balance that would round to zero is shown as <€0.01. Rounded daily limits may not add up to the displayed monthly total.
